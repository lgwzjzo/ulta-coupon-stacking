# Ulta coupons: how to stack codes, points and cashback without cancelling one out

The usual Ulta coupon experience goes like this: you find a code, get excited, load up a cart, then watch it fail on the two things you actually wanted. Or it goes through, and three months later your cashback gets reversed because of a term buried in a dropdown menu.

Both problems come from the same place. Ulta runs three separate discount systems — coupons, rewards points and cashback — and they don't all talk to each other. Knowing which one wins in which situation is most of the game.

This is a working guide to that, plus the current coupon types Ulta keeps reissuing and where each of them stops working.

## The three buckets, and why they barely overlap

**Coupons** come off your total at checkout. They're the loudest discount and the most restricted.

**Ulta Beauty Rewards points** accrue on what you spend, then convert to dollars off a future purchase. Nothing about a coupon changes how many points you earn, though some coupons and bonus-point events do exclude the same product categories.

**Cashback** is paid to you later, by a third party, on the order total. It stacks on top of the other two — with one specific catch we'll get to, because it's the trap that catches most people.

The rule of thumb: coupons are judged by Ulta, points are judged by Ulta, cashback is judged by whoever pays it. Different referees, different rulebooks.

## The coupons Ulta keeps reissuing

Coupon codes at Ulta rotate constantly — a code that works today may be dead in five days. What stays stable is the *types* of offers that come around. Here are the ones that recur:

**$3.50 off $15 qualifying purchase.** This is the most common Ulta coupon and has been for years. It's a transaction-level coupon, so it can't be combined with another transaction-level coupon. It typically excludes prestige brands and fragrance entirely, which is why people are surprised when it refuses to apply to a NARS blush.

**10% off for signing up to text alerts.** Text ULTA to 95637 and you get a one-time, online-only code. This one *does* include prestige and fragrance. It excludes Chanel, Dyson, Black Friday deals, Beauty Steals, gift cards and clearance, and it generally has to be used within about a week of receipt.

**20% off when you buy online and pick up in store.** This is the higher-value coupon, and notably it has been issued in versions that include prestige and fragrance. The trade-off is logistical: you commit to a pickup, and the order needs to be collected within a few days of purchase. Exclusions still apply, and it's not valid at Ulta Beauty at Target, Target.com, UB Marketplace transactions or third-party marketplaces.

**Fragrance-specific codes.** Codes like FRAG15 (15% off fragrance) show up regularly, sometimes as printable in-store coupons and sometimes online. If fragrance is your category, these beat the general coupons because the general ones usually exclude it.

**App-download codes.** A 15% off next-purchase code for installing the free Ulta app has been a recurring offer. You text a keyword to the same short code used for alerts to trigger it.

**Gift-with-purchase thresholds.** Not a coupon exactly, but the value is comparable. Ulta regularly attaches multi-piece beauty bags to spend thresholds — $80, $85, $100 depending on the promotion. These usually require at least one non-marketplace item in the bag, are limited to one per customer, and run out.

## Prestige is the wall

The single most common reason an Ulta coupon fails is the prestige split. Ulta stocks both mass brands (Maybelline, NYX, e.l.f., L'Oréal) and prestige (Tarte, MAC, Clinique, Urban Decay, Olaplex, Too Faced, IT Cosmetics, Chanel, Dior, Gucci). A large share of standard coupons excludes prestige outright.

That's not a bug to work around. It means the coupon you're holding is worth much less than its headline number if your cart is prestige-heavy — 20% off a cart that excludes everything you picked is 0%.

The fix is not to hunt harder for a code. It's to shift to a different mechanism for prestige purchases:

- Prestige-specific coupons, which Ulta does issue periodically
- Points multiplier events, which apply to prestige and can outperform a flat percentage
- Beauty Steals, where specific prestige items drop 50%
- Gift-with-purchase thresholds on prestige brands

A 5x points event on a $120 prestige order is worth roughly $9 in future redemption value at typical rates. A 20% coupon that won't apply is worth $0. That comparison is the whole argument.

> **Watch the small print on single-item vs transaction-level coupons.** Ulta's terms note that single-item coupons are subject to the best available deal and can't be combined with other single-item coupons or discounts, and transaction-level coupons can't be combined with other transaction-level coupons. So "stacking two Ulta coupons" mostly isn't a thing.

## Where coupons flat-out don't work

Ulta's own terms list the dead zones fairly consistently. Most coupons are not valid at:

- Ulta Beauty at Target and Target.com
- UB Marketplace transactions and purchases through third-party marketplaces, including Ulta's TikTok Shop
- Auto-replenish orders
- Purchases made outside the United States
- Gift card purchases, and on clearance
- Prior purchases — no retroactive adjustments

Also worth knowing: the $35 free-shipping threshold is a plain policy, not a promotion. Diamond members get it at $25 instead. Pickup is free either way, which is often the better move when your cart is $28.

## Time the purchase, don't just find a code

The biggest discounts at Ulta aren't coupons at all — they're the semi-annual events.

**21 Days of Beauty** runs in March and September, with daily and weekly Beauty Steals at 50% off across prestige and mass brands, plus hair care and tools. The most recent fall edition ran from late August through mid-September, and included free same-day delivery on orders of $35 or more during the event window. Daily Steals run 24 hours, from midnight to 11:59pm CT, so the item you want is either on sale today or it isn't.

**Jumbo Love** (the liter sale) lands twice a year, around the end of December and again in late June, with up to 40% off large-format hair care. **Love Your Skin** runs in January and August. **Perfect Pairs**, a buy-two-same-brand hair promotion with duos starting around two for $20, tends to run in spring.

If you're buying something you buy anyway — shampoo, mascara, your one serum — moving the purchase into one of those windows beats any coupon you'll find. Prestige brands, especially, are more reliably discounted through Beauty Steals than through codes.

## Adding cashback on top — and the one rule that kills it

Here's the layer most coupon guides skip. Ulta is a live merchant on **ShopBack**, a cashback platform that launched in the US in 2025 and now works with over 2,000 retailers, Ulta included. The current listed rate on Ulta Beauty is **3% Cashback**.

Three percent on a $200 haul is $6. Not life-changing on its own. When it lands on top of a 20% coupon and a decent points haul, though, it's the difference between a good order and an obviously good order.

The mechanics, as listed on the merchant page:

- **Tracked in about 2 days, confirmed in about 120 days.** Tracking and confirmation are two different events. Tracking means the platform sees the transaction; confirmation is what actually makes the cashback payable. Four months is a long tail, so don't plan around the money arriving quickly.
- **Rate can be adjusted.** Your cashback may initially track at a different rate and be corrected once the transaction details are confirmed. That cuts both ways.
- **Exclusions are real.** No cashback on Dyson products, gifts with purchase, samplers and mystery bags, e-gift cards, fees, taxes, shipping, or — this is the important one — **purchases made with vouchers or promo codes not featured on the platform**.
- **Returns get prorated, cancellations get zero.** Returned, cancelled, refunded or exchanged purchases don't earn. Partial returns are adjusted.

That promo-code clause is the trap. A generic 20% code you found somewhere else can be worth more than 3% cashback, or less, depending on your cart total — and you can't always have both. On a $50 order, 20% off is $10 and 3% cashback is $1.50, so the outside code wins. On a $40 order with a $3.50 off coupon, the coupon is $3.50 and cashback is $1.20 — closer than you'd think. Run the numbers per order instead of assuming.

There's also a session rule that people trip over constantly. For Ulta specifically, you need to clear your cart, close the Ulta app, then open the cashback platform and complete the purchase from there. Every single time. The platform also asks you to accept all cookies on Ulta's pages, skip adblockers and VPNs, avoid clicking through from ads or other extensions, and restart from the platform if a payment fails.
